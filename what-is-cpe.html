Discipline Overview

What is Computer Engineering?

Discover the intersection where physical hardware engineering meets modern software intelligence.

Bridging Hardware & Software

Computer Engineering (CpE) is a dynamic discipline that integrates electrical engineering and computer science to develop cutting-edge computer hardware and integrated software solutions.

Engineers in this field design microprocessors, embedded devices, circuit architectures, and computing networks—focusing on making modern technology smarter, faster, and more efficient.

Core Focus Areas

  • Embedded Systems & IoT: Microcontroller programming and real-world physical automation.
  • Digital System Design: Integrated circuits, VLSI, and digital logic architecture.
  • Software & Networks: Low-level systems programming, networking, and data structures.
  • Robotics & AI: Autonomous physical systems and hardware-accelerated computing.
