Computer Engineering (CpE)

What is Computer Engineering?

Computer Engineering (CpE) is a discipline that integrates electronic engineering and computer sciences to design, develop, and test computer systems and hardware components.

Key Areas of Study:
  • Hardware Design: Microprocessors, embedded circuits, and IoT devices.
  • Software Development: Firmware, operating systems, and network protocols.
  • System Integration: Combining hardware and software for specialized technology solutions.
