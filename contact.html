Frequently Asked Questions

Computer Engineering integrates both hardware electrical design and software development. While Computer Science focuses heavily on software theory and algorithms, CpE bridges hardware microprocessors, IoT, and embedded systems with modern software applications.

Graduates can pursue careers as Embedded Systems Engineers, Software Developers, Network Architects, Cybersecurity Specialists, IoT Engineers, Robotics Developers, and IC Design Specialists across tech, telecommunications, and manufacturing sectors.

SCPES (Society of Computer Engineering Students) is the official recognized student organization for Computer Engineering students at the University of the East. It conducts seminars, academic workshops, social events, and competitions.
