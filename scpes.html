About the Organization

SCPES is the official student organization for Computer Engineering (CpE) students at the University of the East - Manila. We aim to foster academic excellence, technical proficiency, and camaraderie among future engineers by providing platforms for innovation and leadership.

The Executive Board leads the SCPES community by organizing technical seminars, managing internal and external affairs, facilitating career development, and handling logistics for college-wide events. Each officer specializes in a crucial area to ensure CpE students have a supportive and enriching college experience.

Computer Engineering integrates electrical engineering and computer science. CpE students at UE Manila engage in hands-on projects involving software development, hardware architecture, embedded systems, networking, and robotics to solve modern technological challenges.
