Opportunities & Careers

Computer engineers shape the digital world. Discover where a degree in CpE from UE can take your career.

Embedded Systems

Design and program firmware for microcontrollers, smart devices, automotive systems, and IoT devices.

  • Firmware Engineer
  • IoT Solutions Developer
  • Robotics Engineer

Software & Systems

Develop system-level applications, operating systems, full-stack software, and cloud interfaces.

  • Software Engineer
  • Systems Analyst
  • DevOps Specialist

Network & Security

Build and secure enterprise networks, telecommunications infrastructure, and cloud data systems.

  • Network Architect
  • Cybersecurity Analyst
  • Cloud Infrastructure Admin

AI & Hardware

Architect digital integrated circuits, FPGA systems, and hardware accelerators for artificial intelligence.

  • VLSI Design Engineer
  • AI Hardware Specialist
  • Semiconductor Engineer
